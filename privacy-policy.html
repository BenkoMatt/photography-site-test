Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Jenna Lynn Photography ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information collected through our website at photographybyjennalynn.com.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name(s), email address, phone number, session type, preferred date, location, and your message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss your photography session.

We do not collect or store payment information through this website. All payments are handled separately and securely.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide through our contact form to:

- Respond to your inquiry
- Discuss and plan your photography session
- Send you gallery links and session details
We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third parties except as described below.

Third-Party Services

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service that delivers your inquiry to our email. Your information is transmitted to Formspree for this purpose and is stored in accordance with their privacy policy, available at formspree.io/privacy. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third parties.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. We do not use analytics tracking, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking pixels.

Photography Sessions

Photos from your session are stored in a password-protected online gallery. Access to client galleries is provided via private links shared with you. We retain session photos for the duration needed to deliver your gallery and fulfill our service obligations.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have shared with us. To make a request, email us at gallerybyjennalynn@gmail.com.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at gallerybyjennalynn@gmail.com.
